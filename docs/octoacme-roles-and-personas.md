# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Manager / Team Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Team Leads guide engineering delivery, team health, and capacity so the team can meet product goals sustainably.

### Responsibilities
- Plan team capacity, coach engineers, and oversee technical delivery
- Partner with Project Managers on schedules, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Align with Product Managers on scope feasibility and trade-offs
- Support Developers with prioritization, execution, and escalations
- Surface delivery constraints and progress to relevant stakeholders

### Goals
- Enable predictable, sustainable delivery
- Help Developers grow and resolve blockers quickly
- Balance product commitments with team capacity and technical health

### Typical Communication
- Capacity and dependency reviews with Project Managers
- Scope and feasibility discussions with Product Managers
- One-on-ones, team planning, and delivery check-ins with Developers

---

## Technical Architect / Staff Engineer

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Staff Engineers provide technical direction and help teams make sound architecture and integration decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate architecture, integration patterns, and non-functional requirements
- Collaborate with Developers on implementation approaches, reviews, and technical risks
- Advise Product Managers on technical trade-offs and sequencing
- Work with Project Managers to identify architecture dependencies and schedule risk
- Guide quality and security considerations throughout delivery

### Goals
- Keep systems secure, scalable, maintainable, and interoperable
- Enable consistent engineering decisions without slowing delivery
- Reduce technical risk through early alignment

### Typical Communication
- Architecture proposals and technical decision records
- Design reviews and implementation discussions with Developers
- Scope and sequencing conversations with Product Managers and Project Managers

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers shape accessible, usable product experiences through research, interaction design, and visual design.

### Responsibilities
- Map user journeys and create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Partner with Product Managers to translate user needs into outcomes and acceptance criteria
- Collaborate with Developers on feasible, accessible implementation details
- Coordinate with Project Managers on design milestones, review timing, and dependencies
- Validate designs with users and share findings with the delivery team

### Goals
- Make product experiences useful, clear, and accessible
- Reduce usability issues through early validation and close delivery collaboration
- Keep design decisions connected to user and product outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Research findings, prototypes, and design handoff notes
- Design reviews with Developers and Product Managers
- Milestone and dependency updates with Project Managers

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Platform Engineers provide the tooling and operational foundations that help teams build, release, and run software reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation, environments, and observability
- Partner with Developers on build, test, and release workflows
- Advise Product Managers on operational trade-offs that affect release scope or timing
- Coordinate with Project Managers on release plans, dependencies, and operational risks
- Confirm monitoring, rollback, and support readiness before releases

### Goals
- Make delivery pipelines reliable and repeatable
- Improve service availability and production visibility
- Reduce deployment risk and manual operational work

### Typical Communication
- Pipeline and environment documentation for Developers
- Release-readiness and risk updates with Project Managers
- Operational trade-off discussions with Product Managers

---

## Security & Compliance Specialist

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Specialists help teams identify and address security, privacy, and regulatory risks throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Perform threat modeling and advise on security controls and compliance obligations
- Work with Developers and Technical Architects on secure design and implementation
- Help Product Managers understand risk implications of scope and product decisions
- Keep Project Managers informed of findings, mitigations, and risk-register updates
- Support incident readiness and evidence collection for applicable controls

### Goals
- Protect users, systems, and sensitive data
- Address compliance requirements without late delivery surprises
- Make security risks and mitigations clear to the team

### Typical Communication
- Threat models, control guidance, and security review findings
- Risk and mitigation updates with Project Managers
- Requirement and trade-off discussions with Product Managers and Developers

---

## Data Analyst / BI Analyst

### Role Summary
Data and BI Analysts turn product and operational data into reliable measures and insights that support decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define metrics, establish baselines, and build dashboards or analyses
- Partner with Product Managers to connect success measures to product outcomes
- Coordinate with Project Managers on reporting needs, cadence, and delivery milestones
- Work with Developers on event instrumentation, data quality, and implementation constraints
- Explain findings and limitations to the team so decisions use sound evidence

### Goals
- Make product outcomes measurable and understandable
- Improve decision-making with trustworthy, timely insights
- Identify trends and opportunities for iteration

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and dashboards for the delivery team
- Outcome reviews with Product Managers
- Reporting and milestone updates with Project Managers

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Liaisons bring customer needs and service experience into planning, release readiness, and post-release learning.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback, support trends, and recurring pain points
- Share customer evidence with Product Managers to inform priorities and improvements
- Coordinate with Developers on issue details, reproduction steps, and technical follow-up
- Partner with Project Managers on support readiness, release communications, and escalations
- Close the feedback loop by communicating known issues and tracking post-release outcomes

### Goals
- Help customers adopt products and resolve issues effectively
- Ensure releases are supported and customer communications are timely
- Turn service feedback into actionable product improvements

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and issue reports
- Release and support-readiness check-ins with Project Managers and Developers
- Feedback and prioritization discussions with Product Managers
